# Day 5

**Tag 5: Transit und PKI**

Starts from a fresh dev server. Leaves the transit keys `webshop` and `webshop-sign`, a root CA in `pki/`, an intermediate CA in `pki_int/` with the role `webshop`, and ACME on `pki_int`.

The book works in `~/bao-lab/tag05`.

| File | Book step | Goes to |
|---|---|---|
| `check.sh` | Kontrollpunkt | `bash check.sh` against that day's lab |
| `challenge/webshop-encrypt.hcl` | Challenge, Lösung | stdin of `bao policy write webshop-encrypt -` |

`root_ca.crt`, `int.crt`, `api.crt`, `api.key` and `cert.json` are generated by the drills and are not included.
